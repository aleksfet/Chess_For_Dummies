Learn the Game

Everything you need to take your very first steps in chess.

What is Chess?

Chess is a two-player strategy board game played on a 64-square board. Each player commands an army of pieces with the goal of trapping the other king.

How the Pieces Move

Every piece moves in its own way — pawns step forward, bishops slide diagonally, knights jump in an L-shape, and the queen does it all.

Basic Rules

Players take turns moving one piece at a time. Learn special moves like castling, en passant, and pawn promotion as you go.

How Games Are Won

You win by delivering checkmate — attacking the enemy king so it cannot escape. Games can also end in a draw.

Improvement

Practical ways to keep getting better.

Puzzles and Tactics

Solving puzzles trains your eye to spot forks, pins, and checkmates. A few puzzles a day makes a big difference.

Endgames

The endgame is when few pieces remain. Knowing basic checkmates and pawn endings helps you finish winning positions.

Reviewing Games

Looking back at your games — wins and losses — shows you what went well and which mistakes to avoid next time.

Playing Online

Free websites let you play opponents at your level anytime. Regular practice is the fastest way to improve.

OTB Chess and Ratings

Understanding real-world chess and how skill is measured.

What is OTB Chess?

OTB means "over the board" — playing chess in person with a physical board, instead of online. It's the classic way to compete.

USCF and Tournaments

The US Chess Federation runs official tournaments in America. Joining lets you earn an official rating and play rated games.

Elo Ratings

The Elo system gives every player a number that reflects their strength. Win against stronger players and your rating climbs.

FIDE and International Chess

FIDE is the world chess organization. It awards titles like Grandmaster and oversees international competition worldwide.

Chess Legends

Some of the greatest and most influential players in history.

MC

Magnus Carlsen

World Champion & All-Time Great

Widely considered the strongest player ever, Magnus reached the world's highest rating. He is famous for grinding out wins from quiet positions.

GK

Garry Kasparov

Dominant World Champion

Kasparov ruled the chess world for nearly two decades. His aggressive style and famous match against the computer Deep Blue made him a legend.

BF

Bobby Fischer

American Genius

Fischer became a global icon by winning the 1972 World Championship. His brilliance helped spark huge interest in chess around the world.

JP

Judit Polgar

Greatest Female Player Ever

Judit Polgar shattered records and beat many top male players in her career. She proved chess greatness has nothing to do with gender.

HN

Hikaru Nakamura

Grandmaster & Streamer

A top grandmaster and speed-chess wizard, Hikaru introduced millions of new fans to the game through his popular streams and videos.
